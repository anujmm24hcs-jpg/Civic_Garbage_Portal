How CivicClean Works

Four simple steps to report and resolve garbage dumping issues.

1

Report

Citizen uploads a garbage photo and provides details.

2

Locate

GPS coordinates identify the exact dumping location.

3

Resolve

Admin reviews the complaint and coordinates the cleanup.

4

Verify

Admin uploads the cleanup photo after the garbage is removed.
